GET READY

Table setup

1 Choose your camera

Video only. No microphone, uploads or cloud processing. Modes are requests, not guarantees.

2 Find the board

Smart setup is the default. It looks for the round playing surface, 20 hole and scoring rings automatically. No calibration clicks or puck are needed. You only confirm the overlay if the fit looks right.

Manual fallback

Use this only if smart setup cannot confidently find your board. The frozen-image walkthrough still supports undo, magnification and preview confirmation.

Not calibrated

3 Teach it your disc colours

Clear all discs and hands, save the empty board, then place one puck from each team and sample its solid centre. After that, puck tracking runs continuously—there is nothing to start for each shot.

Waiting for calibration

4 Automatic assistance

Once tracking is ready, the app watches continuously for puck movement and board settlement. Clear, settled board scores update automatically. Frame gaps, obscured pucks and close lines hold the score for review. Possible 20s are flagged; confirm them with the +20 button.

Waiting for disc tracking

Confirmed 20s: A 0 · B 0

Automation with an abstain path.

Settled-board scoring and clips can run automatically. If tracking loses a puck, frames gap, or a line/20 call is uncertain, the app flags review instead of inventing certainty. Legal/foul decisions remain separate.
